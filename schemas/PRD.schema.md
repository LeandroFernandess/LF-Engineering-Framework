# PRD Schema

## Purpose

Canonical structure for product requirement documents produced by the Product Owner Agent.

## Required Sections

- Product Overview
- Problem Statement
- Goals
- Non-Goals
- Users and Stakeholders
- Functional Requirements
- Non-Functional Requirements
- MVP Scope
- Acceptance Criteria
- Risks and Assumptions
- Open Questions
- Handoff Notes

## Validation

- Requirements must be testable.
- MVP boundaries must be explicit.
- Assumptions must be labeled.
- Technical implementation details should be deferred to `ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- When user research informs a product decision, reference its method, date,
  evidence scope and limitations in relevant PRD sections. Do not include
  identifying participant data or present synthetic responses as participant
  evidence; no separate research artifact is required by default.
